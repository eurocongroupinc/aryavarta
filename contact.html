What to include in your enquiry

Better information produces a better proposal.

For faster response, include the material grade or spare-part specification, expected quantity, pickup and delivery location, delivery urgency, storage needs, GST state, and any compliance documents required by your procurement team.

  • For raw materials: grade, monthly volume, destination plant, and preferred delivery schedule.
  • For spares: machine model, part number, current failure mode, and required delivery date.
  • For logistics: cargo type, route, weight, packaging, loading point, and delivery window.
  • For warehousing: SKU profile, average inventory, dispatch frequency, and storage restrictions.
  • For recycling: material category, approximate volume, location, and documentation requirements.
