Industrial resource management with a Kerala headquarters and a national operating horizon.

Aryavarta Industrial Resources Private Limited is a newly incorporated industrial solutions provider focused on sourcing, supplying, and managing raw materials, equipment components, freight, warehousing, and circular-economy programs for Indian industry.

Company bio

Built for the industrial buyers behind India's manufacturing expansion.

Headquartered in Kochi, Kerala, Aryavarta supports SMEs and large enterprises that need disciplined procurement, dependable supplier networks, and practical logistics coordination. The company combines raw-material sourcing, technical spares support, warehousing, freight forwarding, and recycling services under one operating platform.

Our model is intentionally service-heavy. Customers can engage us for one purchase order, a quarterly supply plan, a multi-location warehousing program, or a broader advisory mandate for factory setup and vendor development. We aim to reduce uncertainty in industrial supply by improving traceability, documentation, delivery coordination, and responsible resource utilization.

Mission

To deliver reliable, transparent, and sustainable industrial resource solutions that empower Indian manufacturing.

Vision

Become a top-5 industrial aggregator in India by 2030 with a net-zero supply chain.

Management approach

Lean founding team, structured operating controls.

As a 2026-incorporated company, Aryavarta is positioned as a focused industrial aggregator rather than a legacy conglomerate. Its strength is a management model built around practical accountability: supplier due diligence, freight coordination, working-capital discipline, and branch-level operating visibility.

The leadership profiles are intentionally presented with experience, responsibilities, appointment dates, and board roles so prospective clients can quickly understand who owns commercial execution, operations, and finance governance.

Core sectors

Where Aryavarta operates.

The company focuses on industrial categories where procurement reliability, freight execution, storage discipline, and compliance documentation directly affect plant continuity.

Ferrous and non-ferrous metals

Iron ore, coal, bauxite, limestone, and related resource streams for manufacturing, processing, and infrastructure-linked industries.

  • Supplier identification
  • Material allocation
  • Contract and dispatch coordination

Industrial chemicals and consumables

Consumables and process inputs for plants that require predictable procurement cycles and safe documentation practices.

  • Vendor evaluation
  • Quality document tracking
  • Periodic consumption planning

Heavy machinery spare parts

Crusher parts, conveyor belts, bearings, motors, and rotating equipment support for industrial sites.

  • Cross-reference support

  • Maintenance coordination

Logistics, 3PL, and circular economy

Freight forwarding, warehousing, bonded storage, e-waste recovery, and zero-waste process improvement.
